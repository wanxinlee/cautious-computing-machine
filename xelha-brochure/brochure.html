Post-Treatment Care

1
Avoid strong irritation to the skin

On the day of your XE'LHA Peel treatment, use only a gentle cleanser. For the following three days, avoid products containing alcohol, or vitamin A and C.

2
Keep skin hydrated and nourished

The treatment opens the skin's inner pathways, so we recommend replenishing moisture and nutrients afterwards.

3
Avoid other skin-renewal treatments for one week

Having other resurfacing treatments, or using vitamin C, within one week after XE'LHA Peel may cause skin damage.
